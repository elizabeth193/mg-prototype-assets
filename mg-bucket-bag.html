THE BUCKET BAG

In 2013, Rachel Mansur and Floriana Gavriel introduced a bag with no logo, no hardware, and a drawstring closure. Vegetable-tanned leather on the outside. A different color inside.

The design was simple by intention. The response was not.

Within months there were waitlists. Within three years, two CFDA awards. Business of Fashion called it the first post-recession It bag. The V&A added it to their permanent collection. The silhouette has remained in production ever since.

Dreamt up in New York City and made with artisans in Italy, it is still made the same way.

THE MATERIAL

Vegetable-tanned leather is a living material. In the first weeks it is structured. Over months it softens and takes shape. Exposure to light and handling deepens the tone.

The surface marks. That is not a flaw. It is a record of use. Every bag becomes specific to the person carrying it.

This is what made to last means.
